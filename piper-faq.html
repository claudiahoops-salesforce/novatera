About NovaTerra

Who is NovaTerra?
NovaTerra is a German industrial technology company founded in Stuttgart in 1987. We design and manufacture precision laser cutting, bending and welding machines — and the Smart Factory software that connects and optimises them. We have over 4,200 machines installed in 38 countries, with offices in Stuttgart, Munich, London and Chicago.
What industries does NovaTerra serve?
Our machines and software are used in automotive (body parts, battery enclosures), aerospace (titanium and aluminium components), medical technology (surgical instruments, implants), sheet metal job shops, and general manufacturing. If you're cutting, bending or welding metal, we have a solution for you.
Where are NovaTerra machines manufactured?
All NovaTerra machines are designed and manufactured in Stuttgart, Germany. We invest 18% of annual revenue in R&D — one of the highest ratios in the industry. Every machine ships with a 2-year performance guarantee.
Does NovaTerra serve customers outside Germany?
Yes. We have active customers in 38 countries including the UK, Netherlands, Switzerland, USA, and across Southeast Asia. Our UK and US offices handle local sales, installation and service. Demos are available in German and English.

Machines & Systems

What machines does NovaTerra make?
Our current machine portfolio includes: TerraLaser 3060 (2D fiber laser cutting, 12 kW, 3000×6000mm working area), TerraBend 5000 (CNC press brake, 320 t, 4100mm working length, automatic tool change), and TerraWeld Cell (6-axis robotic laser welding cell, 4 kW). All machines integrate with the NovaTerra Smart Factory software suite.
What materials can the TerraLaser 3060 cut?
The TerraLaser 3060 cuts mild steel (up to 30mm), stainless steel (up to 20mm), aluminium (up to 25mm), copper, brass, and titanium. The 12 kW fiber beam source is optimised for high-reflectivity materials — no CO₂ laser needed.
What is the TerraBend 5000's angle accuracy?
The TerraBend 5000 achieves ±0.1° angle accuracy — with AI-assisted springback compensation that adapts automatically to material batch variation. First-part-correct bending without test bends, even on new materials.
How long does machine installation take?
Installation and commissioning typically takes 3–5 days for a single machine, including AssetIQ connection and operator training. Our application engineers handle everything on-site. Time to first production part is typically under 1 week.
What warranty comes with NovaTerra machines?
All NovaTerra machines come with a 2-year performance warranty covering parts and labour. Extended warranty packages are available up to 5 years. Remote diagnostics via TerraConnect ensures a service response within 2 hours for critical issues.

Smart Factory Software

What is the NovaTerra Smart Factory suite?
The Smart Factory suite consists of three products: AssetIQ (real-time sensor monitoring and anomaly detection), MaintainAI (predictive maintenance and automated work order creation), and TerraOS (full production orchestration — job scheduling, material flow, reporting). All three products work together and connect to your existing ERP and CMMS systems.
Do I need to buy NovaTerra machines to use the software?
AssetIQ and MaintainAI can connect to any machine with an OPC-UA, MQTT or Modbus interface — including machines from other manufacturers. TerraOS is designed to work with any production environment. However, NovaTerra machines include native AssetIQ integration out of the box.
How does MaintainAI predict failures?
MaintainAI trains machine learning models on your machine's sensor history — vibration, temperature, pressure, current draw — and identifies the patterns that precede failures. Predictions are delivered with a confidence score and recommended action. The models retrain automatically as new data arrives, improving accuracy over time. Average prediction accuracy across our customer base is 87%.
How far in advance can MaintainAI predict failures?
The prediction horizon depends on the failure mode and the volume of historical data. Typical predictions are 7–14 days ahead. For slow-developing issues (bearing wear, seal degradation), MaintainAI regularly delivers 30-day advance warnings. This gives teams time to schedule maintenance in planned downtime windows.
How quickly can we go live with AssetIQ?
For NovaTerra machines, AssetIQ is pre-configured and typically live within the same day as machine installation. For third-party machines, our integration team connects sensors and configures dashboards within 1–2 weeks. Most customers see their first anomaly detection alert within the first week of go-live.
Is the Smart Factory software cloud-based or on-premise?
Both options are available. The standard deployment is cloud-based (hosted on Azure, with data residency in the EU). On-premise and private cloud deployments are available for Enterprise customers with specific data sovereignty requirements. SOC2 Type II certified.

Pricing & Packages

How is the Smart Factory software priced?
Software is priced per connected asset per month. The Starter package (up to 10 assets) starts at €800/month and includes AssetIQ monitoring and basic alerting. The Professional package adds MaintainAI and TerraOS integration from €2,400/month. Enterprise pricing is available for customers with 25+ assets or multiple sites — contact our team for a custom quote.
Are there setup or implementation fees?
For standard AssetIQ deployments on NovaTerra machines, there are no additional setup fees — configuration is included. For third-party machine connections and TerraOS implementations, a one-time professional services fee applies depending on complexity. Our team will scope this before any commitment.
What is the typical ROI timeline?
Based on customer data, the average ROI payback period for the Smart Factory suite is under 9 months. The biggest drivers are eliminated unplanned downtime, reduced emergency maintenance costs, and improved OEE. Nexora Automotive achieved 4.1x ROI in year one. PrecisionMetal AG saved €180,000 in the first 12 months.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. We offer a 30-day free pilot of AssetIQ on up to 3 connected assets. This includes full setup support from our application engineers, and you keep all data and configurations when you convert to a paid plan. Ask our team to set this up during or after your demo.

Demo & Sales Process

What happens in a NovaTerra demo?
Our standard demo is 30 minutes and covers three things: (1) a live AssetIQ dashboard connected to a real machine feed, (2) a MaintainAI walkthrough showing how failure predictions are generated and actioned, and (3) a TerraOS production overview. We tailor the demo to your specific industry and pain points. No generic slides — just your scenario.
How do I book a demo?

Who should attend the demo?
The most productive demos include someone from production operations (Head of Production, Plant Manager, Maintenance Manager) and someone from finance or IT if an ERP integration is likely. We keep it small — typically 2–4 people — so the conversation stays focused and practical.

If the pilot results are positive, we move to a full commercial proposal. The whole process from first demo to signed contract typically takes 4–8 weeks.

Integrations

Does NovaTerra integrate with Salesforce?
Yes — TerraOS has a native Salesforce integration that syncs asset records, service cases, and field service work orders in real time. When MaintainAI creates a maintenance work order, it automatically creates a Salesforce Case and assigns it to the relevant field service resource. Asset health data is visible directly within Salesforce.
Does it integrate with SAP?
Yes. TerraOS integrates with SAP PM (Plant Maintenance) for work order synchronisation, SAP ERP for order and material management, and SAP S/4HANA via standard APIs. Nexora Automotive runs a full SAP integration across their 5 sites.
What machine protocols are supported?
AssetIQ supports OPC-UA, MQTT, Modbus TCP/IP, and Siemens S7 natively. For older machines, our edge gateway hardware can translate proprietary protocols. We support 500+ sensor types out of the box.
Can AssetIQ connect to non-NovaTerra machines?
Yes. AssetIQ is machine-agnostic. We have customers running AssetIQ alongside machines from TRUMPF, Amada, Bystronic, Trumpf, and Mazak. As long as the machine has an OPC-UA or Modbus interface, or we can attach sensors to it, AssetIQ can monitor it.

Support & Service

What support is included with NovaTerra machines?
All machines include 2-year warranty with TerraConnect remote diagnostics. Standard support provides email and phone response within 4 hours during business hours. On-site service response is within 24 hours for critical issues (8 hours for Enterprise customers). Spare parts are stocked regionally for 2-hour delivery in Germany, UK, and North America.
Is training included?
Yes. Machine operator training (1 day on-site) and maintenance training (1 day on-site) are included with every machine. AssetIQ and TerraOS onboarding is delivered remotely over 2 sessions. Additional training packages are available for larger teams or refresher courses.
How do I contact support?
For software support: support@novatera.com or via the in-app help centre. For machine service: call our regional service line or raise a TerraConnect alert directly from the machine interface. Emergency line available 24/7 for production-critical issues.
