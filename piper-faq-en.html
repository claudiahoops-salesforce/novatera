Company

What is Vogler Packtech?
Vogler Packtech GmbH was founded in 1962 in Reutlingen and remains a family-owned business in its third generation. The company develops and manufactures filling, cartoning, and process technology for the processing industry. More than 2,800 systems are currently in operation in 44 countries worldwide, primarily in the pharmaceutical, food, and cosmetics segments.
Where are Vogler machines manufactured?
Development and core manufacturing take place at the main plant in Reutlingen. The assembly facility in Krakow, Poland, has carried out final assembly for the European market since 2003. The US location in Charlotte, NC, opened in 2017 and is dedicated exclusively to customer service and regional commissioning.
Which industries does Vogler Packtech serve?
The focus is on four segments: Food & Beverage (filling, cartoning, and labelling technology), Pharma & Life Science (GMP-compliant filling and packaging lines), Cosmetics (filling and dosing technology for viscous products), and contract packers who frequently process multiple product groups on a single line.

Machines

Which machines does Vogler Packtech offer?
The current core range comprises three product lines:
  • VarioFill – Filling machine for liquid and paste products. Available as the standard variant (up to 300 cycles/min) and as the HP-X (up to 600 cycles/min). Contact materials 1.4404 and PTFE, CIP/SIP-capable, FDA-compliant.
  • FlexCase – Cartoning technology for folding and plug-in cartons. Format changeover without tools, speed up to 280 cartons/min, compatible with formats from 40 × 20 mm.
  • PureMix – Process technology to EHEDG guidelines for mixing and homogenisation. Dead-space-free design, Ra ≤ 0.8 µm, all product-contact surfaces in 1.4435.
How quickly can a format changeover be performed on the VarioFill HP-X?
On the VarioFill HP-X, a complete format changeover can be completed in under 8 minutes, provided the new format is already stored in the recipe memory of the machine controller. All format parts are designed as coded exchange units and can be replaced without specialist tools.
Is the VarioFill FDA-compliant?
Yes. The VarioFill is manufactured in all variants with contact materials in 1.4404 (316L) and PTFE. CIP and SIP cleaning are standard. A complete material certification (3.1 inspection certificate to DIN EN 10204) is supplied with each system. The design meets the requirements of FDA 21 CFR for directly product-contact components.
What does EHEDG certification mean?
The EHEDG (European Hygienic Engineering & Design Group) is an international association that develops guidelines for hygienic design in the food and pharmaceutical industries. An EHEDG-certified system meets defined minimum requirements for freedom from dead spaces, cleanability, and surface quality. At Vogler Packtech this means: no dead zones on product-contact surfaces, roughness value Ra ≤ 0.8 µm on internal walls, welds fully penetrated and ground flush.
How long does commissioning a system take?
The duration depends on the machine type and the line integration. As a guideline: VarioFill (single machine) 3 days, FlexCase 3–4 days, PureMix process system 5–7 days. For complete line installations with multiple machines and transfer technology, the schedule is agreed during the project planning meeting.

Software

What is the LineView Suite?
The LineView Suite is the proprietary software platform of Vogler Packtech, introduced in 2021. It consists of three modules:
  • LineView – Real-time line dashboard with OEE analysis, shift reports, and configurable views. Data is collected directly from the machine controller via OPC-UA.
  • PredictCare – Condition-based maintenance module. Evaluates sensor data and issues maintenance recommendations with a forecast horizon of 7 to 30 days.
  • PackOS – Production execution system (MES-Light) with order management, electronic batch records, and a GMP-compliant audit trail to 21 CFR Part 11.
Does the software work with third-party machines?
Yes. The LineView Suite communicates via OPC-UA (IEC 62541) and Modbus TCP/IP. Machines from other manufacturers can be connected provided they support one of these protocols. For older controllers without network connectivity, Vogler Packtech optionally offers edge hardware for signal acquisition. The connection costs for third-party machines are charged as a one-time fee.
How far in advance does PredictCare forecast failures?
PredictCare issues maintenance recommendations with a forecast horizon of 7 to 30 days, depending on machine type, available sensors, and the historical data set. Prediction accuracy increases with growing volumes of operational data. During the first 30 days of operation, outputs should be treated as indicative values.
Is PackOS GMP-compliant?
Yes. PackOS features a complete audit trail to 21 CFR Part 11. All database entries are time-stamped, assigned to a user, and stored immutably. Electronic signatures and dual-approval workflows for critical process steps are configurable. Validation support (IQ/OQ documentation) is available on request.

Pricing

How is the LineView Suite licensed?
Licensing is based on the number of connected systems (assets) per month. Three tiers are available:
  • Starter – from 600 EUR/month for up to 10 assets. Includes LineView with basic OEE reporting.
  • Professional – from 1,800 EUR/month. Includes LineView, PredictCare, and PackOS, with no asset limit.
  • Enterprise – Individual pricing. For multi-site operations, extended SLAs, and deep ERP integration. Terms on request.
All prices are exclusive of statutory VAT and apply to annual contracts.
Is there a free trial period?
Yes. New customers receive a 30-day pilot operation on up to 3 assets at no licence cost. The prerequisite is an existing network connection at the relevant machines. Vogler technicians carry out connection and setup on site during the pilot phase.
What implementation costs arise?
For Vogler machines, no separate implementation costs arise; the software connection is included in the machine delivery. For third-party machines, a one-time integration fee is charged, the amount of which depends on the number of machines, controller type, and the customisation required. The sales team will provide a non-binding cost estimate following a brief technical pre-assessment.

Demo & Sales

What does a Vogler demo cover?
The demo lasts approximately 30 minutes and is structured in three parts: a live view of the LineView dashboard using real production data from a reference customer (anonymised), a guided walkthrough of the PredictCare module using a concrete maintenance forecast, and an individual ROI calculation based on the line availability and maintenance costs you provide.
Who should attend the demo?
Participation by the plant manager or production manager as well as the maintenance manager is recommended. If PackOS is being evaluated as an MES, early involvement of the IT department or the ERP owner is advisable so that interface questions can be addressed directly.
How does the sales process work?
The standard process comprises four steps: (1) demo appointment with requirements analysis, (2) 30-day pilot operation on 3 assets, (3) joint ROI evaluation and draft proposal, (4) final offer and contract signature. Based on experience, the time from demo to signed contract is 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the customer's internal decision cycles.

Integration

Which ERP systems are supported?
Native connectors exist for SAP S/4HANA (BAPI/RFC and REST-based Integration Suite), Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Dataverse API), and Infor LN (ION Bus). For additional ERP systems, a CSV-based interface or REST API connection is available; the implementation effort in each case must be assessed individually.
Which machine protocols are supported?
The LineView Suite supports OPC-UA (IEC 62541), Modbus TCP/IP, MQTT (broker connection), and direct reading from Siemens S7 controllers (S7-300, S7-400, S7-1200, S7-1500) via the S7 communication protocol. Proprietary protocols of older controllers can be adapted via optional edge gateways.
Can PackOS manage multiple sites?
Yes. PackOS is designed from the outset for multi-site operation. Any number of plants with their own user and permissions management can be administered from a single central instance. Cross-site evaluations and consolidated reports are included as standard.

Support

What is included in the machine delivery scope?
Every Vogler system is delivered with a 2-year manufacturer warranty. Also included are: remote diagnostics via VoglerConnect (Vogler Packtech's proprietary remote access system), a one-day operator training for line personnel, and a one-day maintenance training for the maintenance team. Spare parts recommendations and an initial parts package form part of the system documentation.
How quickly does the service team respond?
For standard customers: first response by email or telephone within 4 hours (business days), on-site deployment within 24 hours. Enterprise customers receive a reduced on-site response time of 8 hours and a dedicated contact in the technical customer service team. Differing SLAs can be agreed contractually.
How do I contact support?
The technical customer service team is reachable by email at support@vogler-packtech.de and by telephone via the service number noted in your system documentation. Customers with an active LineView licence can additionally open a service request directly through the in-app help menu in the dashboard; this is automatically linked to the current machine data.

Do you have further questions?

Our sales team answers technical queries and will prepare a non-binding comparative calculation for your specific line configuration on request.
