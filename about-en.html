Company

Founded 1962. Still family-owned.

Vogler Packtech GmbH develops and manufactures packaging machinery for the pharmaceutical, food, and cosmetics industries. The company is not part of a larger group, is not driven by quarterly reporting cycles, and has made its own technology decisions for more than six decades.

1962
year of founding, family-owned to this day

Vogler Packtech grew out of a straightforward gap in the market: in 1962, there was no filling machinery manufacturer in the Reutlingen region able to meet the specific requirements of local food producers. Karl Vogler, a trained mechanical engineer, filled that gap. The first machines were built for two dairies and a cannery; the depth of in-house production was high from the outset. That principle still holds: Vogler Packtech does not source finished assemblies for safety-critical components but manufactures them at its own facility.

Today the product range covers filling and dosing technology (VarioFill), cartoning technology (FlexCase), and process technology to EHEDG guidelines (PureMix). In 2021 the LineView Suite was added as a proprietary software platform that captures machine data, analyses it, and converts findings into maintenance recommendations. The software is developed by the same engineers who are responsible for the machine controls; Vogler Packtech does not separate hardware and software expertise.

1962

Founded in Reutlingen

Karl Vogler establishes the company as a manufacturer of filling machinery for the regional food industry. First production site: Reutlingen-Mittelstadt.

1978

Expansion into cartoning technology

Following the acquisition of a product line from an insolvent competitor, Vogler Packtech adds cartoning technology to its portfolio. All acquired design data is thoroughly revised.

Entry into the pharmaceutical market

First GMP-compliant filling system for Lohmann Pharma, Neuwied. This marks the beginning of systematic in-house development of pharmaceutical-grade design standards.

Opening of a final-assembly facility in Poland for the European market. Development and core mechanical production remain in Reutlingen.

Hygienic design without compromise

All PureMix systems and the VarioFill product line meet the EHEDG requirements for hygienic design. In practical terms: no dead zones on product-contact surfaces, internal walls with Ra ≤ 0.8 µm, fully welded and ground seams. Inspection is carried out by accredited bodies; test reports are supplied with every system. 3.1 material certification to DIN EN 10204 is standard, not an optional extra.

Total cost of ownership, not purchase price

The list price of a Vogler system rarely falls in the lower third of a competitive tender. What makes the calculation work over the operating life: systems from the 2003-to-2008 production years achieve an average documented availability of 97.4 percent (source: customer survey 2023, n = 142). Format changeover on the VarioFill HP-X takes under 8 minutes, reducing changeover times by an average of 64 percent compared with older machine generations. PredictCare customers report a reduction in unplanned downtime of 38 to 52 percent within the first 12 months after go-live.

Software from the same source

The LineView Suite is developed by the same controls engineers who are responsible for the PLC programs of Vogler machines. The result: no interface problems between machine control and data acquisition, no duplicate parameterisation steps. OEE analyses and PredictCare forecasts are based on raw sensor data taken directly from the drive, not on aggregated SCADA values. The same connection model applies to third-party machines via OPC-UA or Modbus.
