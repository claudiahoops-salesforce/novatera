Beverages  ·  Germany

Brauerei Ketterer GmbH & Co. KG: OEE increased from 74% to 91% after replacing the liquid filling line.

Brauerei Ketterer operates a bottle and wheat beer filling line in the Black Forest region. The third-party equipment in use until 2022 averaged an OEE of 74%. Format changeovers to the 0.5-litre wheat beer container took 22 minutes and required four technicians. Unplanned stoppages on the carbonation line caused an average of 1.8 hours of downtime per shift.

After installation of the VarioFill HP-X with integrated LineView connectivity and PredictCare sensor kit, the first complete shift after commissioning reached 89% OEE. Six months later, the reference value of 91% was confirmed. In the first twelve months, PredictCare flagged three impending failures at filling valves and rotary joints, allowing planned remediation within scheduled maintenance windows in each case.

"The 22-minute changeover time for wheat beer had been costing us time every Monday morning for years. Today we are at 5.5 minutes, and without any additional technicians. That was not a marginal improvement; it changed our entire weekly planning."
Tobias Renn, Plant Manager, Brauerei Ketterer GmbH & Co. KG

Products used: VarioFill HP-X, LineView, PredictCare

Pharma  ·  Switzerland

Lohmann Pharma Packaging AG: Batch documentation time reduced from 40 minutes to 8 minutes.

Lohmann Pharma Packaging AG produces powder fillings and child-resistant cartons in Baar (Zug) for three pharmaceutical manufacturers. Prior to the Vogler installation, the GMP audit trail was maintained manually on paper forms. Batch changeover documentation required 40 minutes of stoppage time per campaign. Audit preparation for external inspections took two to three working days.

PackOS took over complete electronic batch documentation directly from the production process. The connection to the customer's LIMS system was live within four weeks. PredictCare has since monitored 214 sensor points on the VarioFill PD-60 and FlexCase CR-8 and has not permitted a single unplanned stoppage in 18 months of operation.

"We had three GMP audits in 18 months, and all three concluded without any findings. That would not have been achievable with the old paper trail. PackOS documents without gaps, and PredictCare has never let us down."
Dr. Karin Sutter, Head of Manufacturing, Lohmann Pharma Packaging AG

Products used: VarioFill PD-60, FlexCase CR-8, PackOS, PredictCare

Cosmetics  ·  Netherlands

Vita Cosmetics Group: 14 SKUs, one robotic system, mispacks reduced by 91%.

The Vita Cosmetics Group packs skincare, haircare and decorative cosmetics products across 14 different carton formats at its facility in Nijmegen. Until 2023, format changeovers between SKUs were performed manually by two technicians, took an average of 35 minutes, and regularly caused mispacks due to incorrectly inserted format parts. The mispack complaint rate was 2.1% of delivered units.

FlexCase SL-6 top-load robots handle insertion for all 14 formats fully automatically. Format parameters are loaded via barcode scan at the start of each job. LineView displays cycle rate and mispack frequency in real time and issues an alert on any deviation. Format changeover time now consistently falls below 9 minutes.

"Before, every format changeover depended on which technician was on shift and whether they knew the format parts. Now the operator scans the job, the robot loads the parameters, and we have not shipped a single mispack since commissioning."
Anke van der Berg, Operations Director, Vita Cosmetics Group

Products used: FlexCase SL-6, LineView

Food  ·  Germany

Alpenmolkerei Berchtesgaden eG: CIP cycle time reduced from 4.5 to 2.8 hours, water consumption down 34%.

Alpenmolkerei Berchtesgaden eG processes up to 280,000 litres of raw milk daily into cheese, butter and yoghurt at its facility. CIP cycles across four previously installed third-party process vessels ran for 4.5 hours per day, systematically limiting production capacity. Verification of cleaning completeness for validation purposes was time- and labour-intensive and could not always be documented without gaps.

The PureMix PM-200 vessels are designed without dead points and optimised for the shortest possible CIP cycles. PredictCare monitors conductivity and temperature in real time during CIP and reports cleaning completeness to the SCADA system in a compliant format. Validation documentation is generated fully automatically.

"Four hours of CIP every day. That was production time we simply did not have. With the PureMix vessels we are at 2.8 hours, and the validation record is generated automatically. Our last external audit explicitly cited our CIP documentation as a benchmark."
Stefan Hofmann, Head of Engineering, Alpenmolkerei Berchtesgaden eG

Products used: PureMix PM-200, PredictCare
