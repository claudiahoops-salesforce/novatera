LineView Suite

Connected.

The Vogler software platform links every machine on a line into a coherent system. Real-time OEE, failure predictions up to 30 days in advance and full ERP integration – without proprietary isolated solutions.

View Software Suite
LineView
Real-time OEE and line status across all machines. 500 ms updates, shift reporting, alarm forwarding to Slack, Teams or SMS.
PredictCare
Predictive maintenance based on machine sensor data. Failure predictions up to 30 days in advance, automatic work orders, spare parts planning.
PackOS
Production orchestration, order sequencing and ERP integration for SAP, Microsoft Dynamics and Infor. Complete traceability for GMP environments.
Hygienic Design

Built for demanding production conditions.

All Vogler machines are manufactured in Reutlingen and fully commissioned before delivery. Tolerances, surface quality and weld seams are documented according to internal inspection plans – not customer-provided checklists.

Industries

Food & Beverage
Liquid dosing, powder filling and cartoning for dairy, sauces, beverages and dry products. HACCP-compliant, CIP-compatible.
Pharma & Life Science
GMP-compliant filling systems for oral liquids, powders and tablets. 21 CFR Part 11-capable controls, complete batch documentation.
Cosmetics & Home Care
High-speed dosing for viscous media, lotions and powders. Format flexibility for short production runs and frequent product changeovers.
Contract Packagers
