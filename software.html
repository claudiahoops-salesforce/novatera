AssetIQ

Every sensor. Every machine.
In real time.

AssetIQ connects to every NovaTerra machine via OPC-UA and streams vibration, temperature, pressure, and current data into a live dashboard — with AI anomaly detection that flags deviations before they become stoppages.

  • 100ms data refresh — sub-second alert latency
  • AI anomaly detection — learns your machine's normal behaviour automatically
  • OEE per machine, per shift, per line — drill from fleet to sensor in 2 clicks
  • Alert routing — Slack, Teams, SMS, email with escalation rules
  • 12 months data retention — replay any event, compare any shift

MaintainAI

Predict failures.
Before they happen.

MaintainAI trains machine learning models on your machine's sensor history and predicts failures up to 30 days in advance — automatically creating work orders, ordering parts, and scheduling technicians before anything breaks.

  • 7–30 day failure prediction horizon with confidence scores
  • Automatic work order creation in Salesforce, SAP, or IBM Maximo
  • Parts forecasting — reduce emergency procurement by up to 60%
  • Technician scheduling — matched by skill, shift and asset priority
  • Continuous model retraining — accuracy improves over time automatically

TerraOS

Full production orchestration.
From order to dispatch.

TerraOS is the production management layer that sits above your machines — scheduling jobs, managing material flow, tracking orders and generating shift reports. Integrates with SAP, Salesforce and all major ERP systems.

  • Job scheduling — automatic sequencing by machine capacity, material and deadline
  • Material flow — tracks sheet inventory, nesting efficiency, offcuts
  • Order management — connected to your ERP, live delivery status
  • Shift reporting — automated OEE, throughput, scrap reports every shift
  • Salesforce integration — service cases, field service, asset records synced live
