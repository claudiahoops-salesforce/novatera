TerraLaser 3060

The fastest fiber laser
in its class.

The TerraLaser 3060 delivers 12 kW of fiber laser power in a rigid gantry frame — engineered for continuous 3-shift operation with automatic nozzle change, pallet changer, and real-time cut quality monitoring via AssetIQ.

  • 12 kW fiber beam source — cuts steel up to 30 mm, aluminium up to 25 mm
  • Automatic nozzle change — zero manual intervention between materials
  • Pallet changer — continuous loading, no dead time
  • AssetIQ integration — live OEE, cut quality scoring, predictive maintenance
  • Remote diagnostics via TerraConnect — service response in under 2 hours

TerraBend 5000

AI-assisted bending.
First part correct.

The TerraBend 5000 combines a 320-tonne hydraulic press with AI-driven angle correction and real-time springback compensation. The result: first-part-correct bending, even on new materials, without test bends.

  • 320 t press force — handles steel up to 20 mm, aluminium up to 15 mm
  • Automatic tool change — 8-station tool magazine, changeover in 45 seconds
  • AI springback compensation — adapts to material batch variation automatically
  • BendAssist offline programming — import from any CAD system
  • MaintainAI integration — hydraulic pressure monitoring, seal wear prediction

TerraWeld Cell

Robotic laser welding.
Complex seams, perfect results.

The TerraWeld Cell combines a 4 kW fiber laser with a 6-axis robot arm and integrated seam tracking. Designed for complex 3D weld geometries in automotive, aerospace and heavy equipment manufacturing.

  • 4 kW fiber laser — deep penetration, minimal heat distortion
  • 6-axis robot — full 3D weld path freedom, ±0.05 mm repeatability
  • Seam tracking — adapts in real time to fixture variation
  • EasyWeld AI — automatic parameter optimisation from first weld
  • Integrated quality camera — 100% inline weld inspection
