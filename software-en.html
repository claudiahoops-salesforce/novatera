LineView

OEE and line status. Live, in any browser.

LineView reads all connected machines via OPC-UA or Modbus and displays the availability, performance, and quality of each unit with updates every 500 milliseconds. The dashboard runs directly in the browser -- no client software, no VPN required.

  • 500 ms data refresh from all machines on the line
  • OEE per machine, per shift, per line with stoppage classification
  • Alert forwarding to Slack, Teams, SMS, and email with configurable escalation rules
  • Compatible with any OPC-UA or Modbus machine, not only Vogler equipment
  • 94 % average OEE at customer sites in the first year of operation

PredictCare

Detect failures 7 to 30 days in advance.

PredictCare continuously analyses vibration, temperature, torque, and pressure data from all connected machines. The ML models retrain automatically on new sensor data and improve their accuracy over time. Average forecast accuracy across all customer fleets currently stands at 87 %.

  • Failure predictions 7 to 30 days in advance with a confidence value per forecast
  • ML models retrain automatically on new sensor data
  • Automatic creation of maintenance orders in SAP PM, Maximo, or a CMMS of your choice
  • Spare parts planning: components are automatically reserved from stock before the predicted failure date
  • Average payback period at customer sites: 9 months

PackOS

Order control, material flow, and shift reports in a single system.

PackOS forms the orchestration layer above your packaging machines. It handles order sequencing, controls material flow, and generates automated shift reports with configurable KPI dashboards. A single instance manages multiple plants simultaneously.

  • Order sequencing, material flow, and campaign planning
  • Native integration: SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Infor LN
  • Full batch traceability for GMP environments (21 CFR Part 11-compatible audit trail)
  • Automated shift reports with configurable KPI dashboards
  • Multi-site capable: one instance for multiple plants
