Filling & Dosing

VarioFill

The VarioFill product line covers liquid and powder products across a wide range of container formats. Both types share the same control architecture, the same service standard, and can be integrated into the LineView Suite.

Liquid filling
VarioFill HP-X

High-speed filling system for aqueous and low-viscosity liquids based on volumetric flow measurement. Up to 600 cycles/min, container sizes from 10 ml to 5 l. Suitable for beverages, pharmaceutical fluids and cosmetics products.

600 cycles/min  ·  10 ml–5 l  ·  12 kW
Powder dosing
VarioFill PD-60

Auger-based powder dosing unit for dose weights from 10 g to 5 kg. Up to 120 bags/min, dust-tight filling, product changeover without cross-contamination. For dry products, bakery goods and pharmaceutical granulates.

120 bags/min  ·  10 g–5 kg  ·  auger principle

Standard features common to both types

Both VarioFill types share the same mechanical platform. This reduces spare parts inventory and training requirements for operators and service personnel.

  • Format changeover in under 8 minutes without tools. LineView records changeover time and logs it per order.
  • FDA-compliant contact materials: 1.4404 (AISI 316L) and PTFE for all product-wetted surfaces and pipework.
  • Integrated checkweigher system with automatic dosing feedback control when deviation exceeds ±0,3 %.
  • CIP-compatible pipework system, fully drainable, with compressed air support for residual product evacuation.
  • LineView integration via OPC-UA: cycle rate, fill weight and fault messages transmitted every 500 ms.

Cartoning Technology

FlexCase

FlexCase combines robot-guided motion control with servo-driven flap closing. The CR-8 is designed for continuous high-throughput lines; the SL-6 addresses flexible pick-and-place applications with changing tray formats.

Continuous cartoning
FlexCase CR-8

Continuous motion machine for folding cartons. A 6-axis SCARA robot handles loading and closing at up to 280 cartons/min. Servo-driven flaps, no mechanical cam drive. Suited for high-volume food and OTC pharmaceutical lines.

280 cartons/min  ·  6-axis SCARA  ·  servo
Top-load cartoning
FlexCase SL-6

6-axis robot with interchangeable gripper system for tray and carton formats. Suitable for irregular products, fragile containers and multi-layer patterns. Format changeover via software, no mechanical tooling required.

6-axis robot  ·  flexible tray formats  ·  tool-free changeover

Standard features of both FlexCase types

The CR-8 and SL-6 use the same control hardware and PackOS interface. A combined service contract is available when both types are deployed in a single line.

  • 6 carton formats changeable without tools. Changeover time is verifiably under 12 minutes, measured and logged by PackOS.
  • Vision system for position detection and completeness checking prior to insertion into the folding carton.
  • Servo-driven flap closing: reproducible adhesive application at every position, no mechanical adjustments after format changeover.
  • PackOS integration: order data, batch number and packaging parameters are transferred directly at order changeover.

Process Technology

PureMix

PureMix mixing systems are designed to EHEDG guidelines: no dead zones, fully drainable geometries, surface finish Ra ≤ 0,8 µm on all product-wetted surfaces. Available in two size classes from 200 l to 15,000 l working volume.

Agitator mixing vessel
PureMix PM-200

Top-entry agitator system with variable-frequency drive. Working volume 200 to 2,000 l, suitable for liquid and semi-viscous products. All weld seams and surface values are documented per vessel and supplied as an acceptance report.

200–2,000 l  ·  EHEDG-certified  ·  CIP/SIP
Inline process vessel, large scale
PureMix PM-15000

Large-volume process vessel with integrated homogeniser for working volumes of 5,000 to 15,000 l. For continuous production processes in beverage, dairy and pharmaceutical facilities. CIP/SIP capability covers the homogeniser circuit in full.

5,000–15,000 l  ·  with homogeniser  ·  EHEDG

Design standards common to both types

Both PureMix types are manufactured in Reutlingen. The acceptance report includes weld seam inspection to DIN EN ISO 5817, surface roughness measurements and a pressure retention test.

  • EHEDG certification: dead-zone-free design, no gaps or undercuts on product-wetted surfaces in accordance with EHEDG type testing.
  • CIP/SIP capability on all vessels, pipework and connections. Complete drainage without residual cleaning required.
  • Materials: 1.4404 (AISI 316L) for standard applications, Hastelloy C-22 for corrosive media such as acetic acid or chlorine-based cleaners.
  • Surface finish Ra ≤ 0,8 µm, electrolytically polished, with a measurement record per batch as part of the delivery documentation.
